THE RAPTURE

Will You Be Ready When Jesus Comes?

Introduction

Friends by every indication the return of our Saviour Jesus Christ is nearer than ever, as written in 1 Corinthians 15:51–52 and 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17. "Listen!" says Jesus, "I am coming soon" (Revelation 22:12 GNT). Many professing Christians are aware of this, yet do not know what they ought to do. Many others remain unaware.

Worse, many churches have abandoned the foundation of the apostles and now operate in error that is another gospel which will ultimately lead to "Had I known" on the last day (Galatians 1:6–9).

But through the revelations of the Holy Spirit in this book, those who are the seed of God — the wise virgins — will read this epistle and come out of error before it is too late.

Jesus said,

"My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me"

— John 10:27

"…and a stranger will they not follow" (John 10:5).

He also warned, "Not everyone that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven" (Matthew 7:21).

That is why He commanded us to be watchful against false prophets and wrong doctrines (Matthew 7:15; 16:6), and Paul warns, "Wherefore let him that thinketh he standeth take heed lest he fall" (1 Corinthians 10:12). Jesus said of the Holy Spirit, "And when he is come, he will reprove the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment" (John 16:8)

The Holy Spirit explains this and more in this book.

Jesus is coming soon. But not everyone calling Him "Lord" will go up (Matthew 7:21). The rapture is for wise virgins only. The foolish will be left behind (Matthew 25:10–12).

In 1 Corinthians, Apostle Paul declared,

"According to the grace of God which is given unto me, as a wise Master builder, I have laid the foundation, and another buildeth thereon. But let every man take heed how he buildeth thereupon. For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ"

— 1Corinthians 3:10–11.
The rapture is not for those building on another foundation — false prophets, false doctrines, or false practices. Here are 5 Signs of Another Foundation that will get you left behind:

1. Another Way to the Father

Jesus said,

"I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me"

— John 14:6

But this generation says there are other ways to the Father, and creates so-called co-redeemers. That is another gospel (Galatians1:8). Another gospel will not get you raptured.

2. Another Baptism Formula

Jesus commanded baptism "in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost" (Matthew 28:19). The apostles obeyed by baptizing in the name of Jesus Christ (Acts 2:38). The name of the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost is Jesus (Colossians 2:9).

But this generation is doing the opposite, not baptizing at all or repeating titles instead of the name that Jesus commanded is disobedience — that is operating in error and it is another gospel. The bride takes His name. Don't be a foolish virgin who says, "It doesn't matter, just believe and trust God."Read through to the end of this book, and you will understand it all clearly

3. Another Set of Ministries

Aside from deacons who serve the church, Apostle Paul gave us five offices known as the fivefold ministry for the perfecting of the saints: apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, and teachers (Ephesians 4:11–12).

But this generation invented extra titles for honour and glory. That is another gospel. The rapture is for the perfected church, not a man-made clergy system (Ephesians 5:26–27). The church is washed by "the washing of water with the Word." False doctrine stains; truth applied from Scripture washes and keeps the church holy (Ephesians 5:27).

4. Another Doctrine: Spirit-Led vs. Believe Only

Jesus said,

"Ye shall be baptized with the Holy Ghost"

— Acts 1:5

Paul explained,"If any man have not the Spirit of christ, he is none of his" (Romans 8:9). No Holy Ghost, no heaven. No Holy Ghost, no rapture. Yet many stop at John 3:16 and say "just believe and trust."

5. Another Sacrifice: Self-Righteous Works

After the cross, Jesus declared,"It is finished" (John 19:30). But this generation adds sacrifices and works for salvation, as if Christ's death was not enough. That is another gospel (Galatians 1:8). Come out of error and be rapture-ready.

But the wise virgins watch everything. The foundation is finished (1 Corinthians 3:11). No man can add to it. The wise virgins build on the finished work with gold, silver, precious stones — the apostles' doctrine (Acts 2:42). The foolish build with wood, hay, and stubble — traditions, wrong doctrines, moralism, and new revelations that contradict the Bible. It will burn (1 Corinthians 3:13). They will be left behind.

Christ's blood finished the payment. The apostles' doctrine finished the pattern. You cannot add to the payment, and you cannot subtract from the pattern.

The wise virgins rest in Jesus' sacrifice and walk in obedience. The foolish trust works to save them, or trust grace to excuse disobedience. The difference is this: the wise virgin loves the truth, seeks it, and obeys it. The foolish virgin says it doesn't matter — just believe. Be wise.

The rapture is coming. Jesus is coming soon. The question is not "when." The question is whether you are ready ?
